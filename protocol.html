The prenatal side

Start it while you are trying, not after.

Most women start a prenatal the week they get a positive. The neural tube closes in the first few weeks, often before a test reads positive at all, which is why folate matters before conception rather than after it.

Molo Prenatal uses 600 mcg of folate as L-5-MTHF, the active methylfolate form, with 1,000 mg of vitamin C, vitamin D, CoQ10 and 50 mg of ginger root. It is built as a preconception foundation. When you get a positive, bring this label to your OB and ask about your iron and choline targets.
